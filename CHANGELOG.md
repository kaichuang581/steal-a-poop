# Changelog

All user-visible changes, newest first.

## 0.7.0

- Music for every place. The calm lofi now plays on the start screen only, and it is a fuller song: eight chords, a walking bass, a written tune and its answer, and a few bars where the drums drop out. The Trading Post has a shop shuffle, a lobby has its own waiting song, Practice, the Tutorial and Counting Sheep (a lullaby) each have theirs, and a game has "Pass the bomb", which plays harder and faster every round as the fuses get shorter and lets go when the game is over. The sound menu says which song is playing. All of it is made live in the browser: no audio files, no hiss.
- The fuse sounds are real sounds. They were one beep at eight pitches, quieter than the music until the last three seconds. Now the wood block knocks tick and tock, the cowbell clanks, the sleigh bells jingle, the barn owl hoots and is answered, the music box plays a lullaby one note a second, the tin toy chirps and the lullaby rings two soft bells; every second can be heard over the music and the last three are louder, higher and doubled. In the Trading Post a card's speaker shows that it is playing, the music steps back for the sample, and with sound switched off it says so.
- The Trading Post is the inside of a shop, and the only way to get most items is a Feed Sack: 250 Wool Coins for one random item. There are three and they never mix: the Oat Sack (bomb skins), the Corn Sack (bursts) and the Barley Sack (fuse sounds). The odds are chalked on a slate (common 62%, uncommon 28%, rare 8%, legendary 2%); a rare or better is guaranteed within 10 sacks and a legendary within 40; a repeat pays Wool Coins back, and a rare or legendary never repeats. Wool Coins come only from playing. Nothing costs money and nothing changes how a game plays.
- A sack is opened on the fair machine: pull the lever and the reel spins down to what you got. What you get is decided before the reel spins. Now and then the reel comes to rest right beside a rarer item.
- The three sacks stand in crates on a market stall: a striped awning with snow on it, shelves of stock behind, a brass scale, a lantern that moves to the sack you pick, and each sack's name and what you have found on a paper tag.
- Golden Bells, a second currency earned by levelling up, word milestones and Counting Sheep streaks, buy the two items on the Bell Shelf. Missions gives the streak items. The Barn Book shows every item, found or not, where each comes from, and a closer look at any of them: a bomb skin turning on a stand, a burst going off, a fuse sound to listen to.
- Nine of the ten bursts are no longer explosions: when the bomb goes off a haystack, a snow cloud, balloons, a campfire, a clump of heather, an aurora, a little sun, paper lanterns or stars appear where it was. Only Frost still explodes.
- Every bomb skin is its own bomb, with its own shape, its own glow or sparkle, and its own mark left on the floor: Ember is a hot coal that leaves a scorch, Gold leaves coins.
- A beat after a bomb. When a fuse runs out the sheep beside it is knocked dizzy, with stars over its head, and the next sheep's fuse is lit 2.4 seconds later, in solo games and rooms alike. Before, the next clock was already running while the camera was still on the bang, so a 5 or 10 second turn felt like 3.
- The end of a game plays out: the last bang, the winner named on a board, then the barn doors, then the results.
- A profile page, from your name plate or the menu: your stats (different words, wins, longest word, practice and Counting Sheep bests, night streak), the top 100 players by XP, where you stand if you are not among them, and the place to change your name.
- Smoother on sharp screens: the barn table now holds 60 frames a second at resolutions where it ran at about 40. Night games are a little brighter.
- Signed in, the server no longer takes a browser's word for everything: a Counting Sheep streak can rise by one night a day, and the first time a browser's wallet is brought into an account it brings at most a week's streak, two rare items, one legendary and 100 Golden Bells.

## 0.6.4

- Real people first: Quick play (the old Start now) puts you in a queue with other players, with a chat while the barn fills. Create lobby opens a barn of your own, friends only or public, with a party code and a link to send; the host picks the game length there, and a game needs only two people (farm sheep take the empty seats). Join party takes a friend's code. A table against the bots is now Custom game. The lobby is a sheep pen in the snow: everyone who joins stands inside the fence until the host opens the gate

## 0.6.3

- No swears, slurs or sexual words anywhere: they are gone from the word list, so no one (and no bot) can play one at a table, in a room or in Counting Sheep, and the same list keeps them out of names

## 0.6.2

- The Barn Bazaar is now the Trading Post

## 0.6.1

- The Barn Bazaar is on the main menu and opens as its own page: your level and coins at the top, then every item under its heading (Bomb skins, Bursts, Fuse sounds, Milestones) in one scroll, with tabs that jump down the page. The browser's Back button or Esc returns to the farm
- Signed in, your Wool Coins, XP and items are kept on your account and follow you to any device. The first time you sign in, this browser's wallet is brought in (up to 1,000 coins)

## 0.6.0

- The Barn Bazaar: a shop on the start screen (the bag button, with your Wool Coins on it). Buy bomb skins, burst colours and fuse sounds with Wool Coins. Everything is only for show, every price is shown, and nothing is random or sold for money
- Wool Coins and XP: every finished Quick Game, practice run and Counting Sheep night pays coins and XP. Coins from games stop at 300 a day; levels and milestones pay on top
- Levels and barn titles, from Lamb to Golden Fleece, with a coin bonus each level
- Your word collection: every different word you play is kept, with milestones at 50, 250, 1,000 and 5,000 words, and for words starting with X, Z, Q or J
- Dream streak rewards: the Aurora bomb at 7 nights in a row, the Starlight burst at 30 and the Lullaby fuse at 100. Once earned they are yours for good, even if the streak ends
- Frost creeps in from the screen's edges in the last three seconds of a turn, your words land with a crunch, and on phones a word or a timeout gives a little buzz
- Words with X, Z, Q or J flash gold
- The save code in Settings now carries your Wool Coins, XP and Barn Bazaar items too

## 0.5.0

- New avatar portraits: pixel-art heads for the sheep, pig, cow, goat, horse and chicken, and the sheep is the tab icon
- Word of the day! A new word floats on the start screen every day, with how to say it, what kind of word it is and a few words on what it means. Weekdays are gentler, weekends harder, and it changes at midnight
- The Hunt is gone from the farm
- The loading screen's barn doors are built from voxels like the rest of the farm: planked, painted, iron-hinged, a wreath on one door, snow on the beam and at their foot, and snow falling
- Going into a game, the barn doors swing shut with a thud, a slip says what is being got ready, and they open onto the table. The game waits for them: the table's intro and your first turn start when you can see them, not behind the doors
- Security: the game server can no longer be knocked over by a malformed request or message, rooms are harder to flood, a single player's report can no longer get someone banned without a person looking, a parent's "no" to a child's account is final, room names are checked like player names, and nobody but the owner can wear the owner's avatar
- The game will not run inside another website's frame

## 0.4.0

- The Hunt opens on a lobby instead of throwing you straight into a fight: New Pack, Enter Code, Bestiary or back to the farm. Nothing starts until you choose
- Every pack has a five-character code, shown during the fight, on the results card and in what you share. Type a friend's code, or open their link, to fight the very same predators and rules. "Fight this pack again" replays a code and counts which try it is
- Rules teach before they bite: each rule sign says what it means in plain words, with a word that passes and a word that fails. A face-down card warns that a predator brings a secret rule when it's hurt. RULES shows the cards (reading costs time, not a turn)
- As you type, the Hunt tells you if your word would break a rule, isn't a word, or was already used, and the rule's tag turns red. Sending it anyway still breaks the rule
- When a run ends, a screen tells you what got you (the clock, a broken rule, or the predator's strikes) and a word that would have worked, then lets you fight the pack again, take a new pack, or see your results
- The results are one picture made to be screenshotted: your sheep and the predator, predators beaten and damage, your best word, the pack code and which try, with stamps for a new best or walking home
- After beating a boss you can walk home with your win, or hunt on. Leaving mid-run asks first
- The bestiary: six cards, silhouettes until you meet each predator, with how many you've beaten, the highest level and your biggest hit. Flip a card for when you first met it and its signature move
- A save code in Settings carries your records (Hunt bests, the bestiary, your dream streak and more) to another browser: copy it here, paste it there, see what will change, then restore
- The Hunt keeps a log of your last 50 runs on this device (how far you got and what ended each run), so the Hunt can be tuned from real play. It never leaves the device; admin can copy it out by hand

## 0.3.4

- The bomb is an ice bomb now: a frozen blue shell capped with frost, a frosted fuse and a cold blue spark, with icy blue seconds on its face
- Each new holder gets the bomb properly: it is lobbed over in a tumbling arc (a fresh one drops from above), thuds onto the floor beside them, skids to a stop and wobbles still, and the holder flinches. Frost spreads under it and cold vapour spills off it while it sits there
- It bursts in ice: a white-out, a ball of frost, glittering crystals, chunks of the shell bouncing across the floor and a freezing mist, with frost creeping in at the screen's edges, the words shattering in ice blue, and a glassy crack and tinkling shards to hear

## 0.3.3

- Stealing is gone: no more red seconds, steal turns or STEAL button. Keep your wool by answering in time, and win more with traps
- The steals leaderboard, stat and coins are retired with it

## 0.3.2

- Every animal avatar is rebuilt to proper farm-animal proportions: longer bodies, heads sized to them, necks that join smoothly, and a horse that stands tallest at the table
- The sheep has a new face: a broad, blunt head held level with its back, small dark eyes, ears out to the sides, and its collar and bell at the neck
- Beady eyes for the sheep, cow, horse, goat and chicken: small and dark, no more goggling
- The cow is bigger, as a cow should be: second only to the horse, with spots you can see
- The chicken is a real hen now: plump and level, head forward on a short neck, a proper beak, big wings and a raised fan tail
- Mouths stay on faces: no more chewing below the chin
- Sweaters, blankets and vests fit the body all round and close at the neck
- Every animal moves like itself: horses and cows lower their necks to graze, the hen pecks and waddles, the pig roots, the goat jumps sideways, and bells swing
- Counting Sheep runs the animal you wear

## 0.3.1

- Counting Sheep's dream is rebuilt: a bright snow lane on a cloud raft, floating islands, hills and mountains, a crescent moon, and the farmer asleep in his own cloud bubble
- Counting Sheep is easier and fairer: fences ask for three letters at most, every fence has plenty of common words that fit, signs read R…N, and a fence offers a word if you stop to think
- The sheep leaps properly: fences, walls, logs and hay bales now cross the lane, and a word typed in time can no longer end in a bump
- Counting Sheep has its own night-time look, and every dialog on the farm is paper in a snowy wooden frame
- Counting Sheep has an ending: clear 30 fences and the farmer drifts off; three bumps wake him, and two bumps no longer end the night

- Fences start further apart and the sheep keeps speeding up, like the offline dinosaur game
- Hay bales turn up later in the run, close behind the fences, for a quick second word
- The result screen shows your night first, then your streak, Share, when the next dream arrives, and Practice
- Shared results show each bump where it happened and link to the game
