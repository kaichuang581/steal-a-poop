# Child safety policy: DRAFT

*Draft for lawyer review. Public statement once approved.*

**Our commitment.** Children may play this game, and we work to keep them safe. We do not allow sexual content, grooming, requests for personal information from children, or any attempt to meet a child off the platform.

## What we do

1. **Chat checks.** Every chat message is checked before anyone sees it. The check looks for sexual content, grooming phrases, and requests for private information. It is a first net, not a verdict. People review what it catches.
2. **Account limits for children.** Accounts for children under 13 need verifiable parental consent before they can chat. [Lawyer: confirm the age limit for each country.]
3. **Reporting.** Any player can report a message. A report about possible child sexual abuse material or grooming is treated as urgent and goes to a person immediately.
4. **Removal.** Accounts that break these rules are banned. Their content is removed from view.
5. **Reporting to authorities.** Where the law requires, we report to the relevant authority, for example the NCMEC CyberTipline in the United States or the equivalent body in other countries. A person confirms each report before it is sent. [Operator: list the bodies per country once registered.]
6. **Preserving evidence.** We keep the relevant messages, account details and context as evidence, as the law requires, and we do not delete them while a legal hold applies.

## What we ask of players and parents

- Do not share personal details, photos, or your location with anyone in the game.
- Tell a parent or trusted adult if someone asks you to keep a secret, asks where you live, or asks for pictures.
- If something worries you, report the message from the chat screen, and tell a parent or trusted adult.

## What we ask of moderators

- Child-safety items are not decided alone. A second person confirms before a report is sent, unless the law requires otherwise. [Operator: decide the procedure.]
- Staff who see this material are trained and have support. [Operator: set this up.]
- Access to evidence is limited to named people.
