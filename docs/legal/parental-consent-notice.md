Your child, [child's nickname], has asked to create an account on Steal a Poop, a free word game with multiplayer rooms and a chat.

**What your child can do with an account.** Play alone or with other players, store game results, and join rooms and chat with other players. The chat is checked before anyone sees it, and any message that breaks our rules is held and reviewed by a person.

**What we collect.** Your child's nickname, email address and date of birth, your email address, and game results. We do not collect your child's location, contacts or photos. [Operator: complete from `privacy-policy.md`.]

**Who can see it.** Other players see your child's nickname, chat messages and game results. Our moderators see held messages and reports.

**Your choices.** You can:
- Give consent, which lets your child chat and store results.
- Refuse, which deletes the request. We will not contact you again.
- Withdraw consent later, which closes or limits the account. Email [contact].
- Ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about your child.
