# Shop and currency (plan, not built)

Status: **coins and cosmetics built (0.6.0); real money not built.** What is built: Wool Coins and XP earned in this browser, the Trading Post (src/skin/shop), levels, word milestones and dream streak items. Not built: Golden Apples or any real-money sale, ads of any kind, and syncing the wallet to an account. Those wait for the server, a payment provider, parental approval and a lawyer. Items marked DECIDE need your answer. Anything that takes real money from a child needs your lawyer's review and a parental approval step before launch.

## Principles
1. **No random paid rewards.** No loot boxes, no gacha, no spin wheels, no "pity" timers. Every paid item is listed with its price, and the player gets exactly that item.
2. **Earned coins never need money.** Coins come from playing. A player who never pays can still get every item in the game, just more slowly.
3. **Paying never changes how the game plays.** Nothing bought with money gives a turn, time, wool or word advantage.
4. **Parents decide money.** Any real-money purchase by a player under the age of digital consent asks a parent first, through a parental gate (a check a child cannot pass by accident) and a parent email approval.
5. **No ads aimed at children.**

## Two currencies
| | Coins | Real money (£/$) |
|---|---|---|
| How you get it | Playing games | Paying, through the app store or payment provider |
| What it buys | Cosmetics and boss-unlock tickets | Cosmetic items and supporter items only |
| Random? | Never | Never |
| Cap | Daily earn cap (see below) | No cap, but every item is listed with its price |

## Earning coins (no money)
- **Finishing a game:** a base amount per game, scaled by how many rounds you lasted.
- **Your first win of the day:** a small bonus.
- **Daily cap:** coins stop being earned after a set amount a day, so the game can't be farmed. Proposed cap: 300 coins a day. DECIDE.
- **Milestones:** first 10 games, first steal (if steals return), first boss beaten. One-time bonuses.
- **Counting Sheep and Hunt:** a small coin payout for each finished night or run, with the same daily cap shared across all modes.

## Spending coins (cosmetics and unlocks only)
| Category | Examples | Price range | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hats and accessories | a bow, a farmer's hat, a rainbow hat | 50 to 300 | Visual only |
| Death scenes | a bouncing exit, a confetti burst | 100 to 400 | Visual only, never changes what happens |
| Avatar extras | a new trail, a name colour | 80 to 250 | Visual only |
| Boss tickets | a ticket to challenge a boss | 150 | Gives a chance to face the boss, not a reward |

Rules:
- Every item shows its price and what it looks like before purchase.
- **Limited items** have an end date that is shown on the item. When it ends, the item stays unavailable for purchase but still owned by people who bought it. DECIDE the limit length.
- **Boss-only items** are unlocked by beating a boss in the progression mode, not bought. They can't be bought for money.

## Buying with real money (cosmetics only)
| Product | Price (proposed) | What you get | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter pack | £1.99 | 300 coins and one hat | One-time |
| Coin pack small | £0.99 | 200 coins | Fixed |
| Coin pack large | £4.99 | 1,200 coins | Fixed |
| Supporter badge | £2.99 | A name badge on the leaderboard, no gameplay | One-time |
| Named farm stall | £1.99 | A decorative sign on your home farm | One-time |

- **No item is sold only for money** if it cannot also be earned or bought with coins. That way, a player who never pays can still get it.
- Coin packs only exist to let a player buy items faster. They don't unlock anything else.
- Prices are proposals for the lawyer and app-store review. DECIDE.

## Options I'd advise against
- **Loot boxes or any random paid reward.** Not in this plan. A gambling pattern for kids.
- **Paid gameplay advantages** (more time, extra wool, hints bought for money). These would make paying players win more games, which is unfair to players who don't pay. Excluded from this plan.
- **Ads aimed at children.** Excluded.

## DECIDE: items you still need to answer
1. Daily coin cap: 300 a day, or another number?
2. Limited items: how long does a limited item stay on sale? One week, one month, or a season?
3. Real-money packs: are these prices right, and do you want the coin packs at all?
4. Should real-money purchases be allowed for players under 13 at all, with a parent's approval, or only for players who are old enough to agree alone?
5. Are boss tickets a good idea, given that they're a chance to face a boss rather than a reward? Or should bosses be unlocked only by playing?

## Requirements before any real-money sale goes live
- Lawyer review of the purchase terms, refund policy and children's data handling.
- A payment provider and app-store rules that allow sales to children, with parental consent recorded.
- A parental gate on every purchase screen.
- A refund route for parents, and a way to see and cancel purchases in the account.
- Tests: coin earning, the daily cap, purchase flow, refund flow, and proof that no item sold for money changes gameplay.

## Build order (once decided)
1. Coins: earning, the daily cap, and a balance shown on the home screen.
2. Shop screen: cosmetics bought with coins, with prices and previews.
3. Boss-only and limited items, with end dates.
4. Real-money packs, only after the requirements above are met.

## Done when
- A player who never pays can earn and buy every cosmetic.
- No real-money product changes how a game plays.
- Every purchase screen has a parental gate, and every real-money sale has a parent approval.
