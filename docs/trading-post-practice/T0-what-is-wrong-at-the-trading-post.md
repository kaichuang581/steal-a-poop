# T0. What is wrong at the Trading Post today, and what changes

Seen in `reference/build-today/shop-*.jpg` (computer 1440 x 900, phone 390 x 844).

## A. The frame

| # | Flaw | Change |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | The Trading Post is a wooden panel floating over a dimmed Home: planks, corner brackets, a knitted trim, a snow-blob title sign with drips. Every other screen has dropped this style | A full-screen place: the market row (`T1`) |
| A2 | The stall is a picture inside the panel and the items are cards under it: two styles on one screen | The stall is the screen. Items stand on it as models, with tags |
| A3 | The panel cuts off its own content at the bottom: item names under "Inside the Oat Sack", the odds' last line, the Bell Shelf's small print, the Barn Book's second row | Each stop fits its screen with nothing to scroll on a computer; phones scroll one paper roll (`T7`) |
| A4 | Five kinds of header piece: a hanging wood sign, a dashed dark wallet box, a round wooden close button, four wooden tabs, a cream level strip | One family: paper tags on a yarn line, wool tiles, one strip (`T7`) |
| A5 | Handwriting font (its "7" reads as a crossed "t"), spaced capitals ("NOT FOUND YET", "COMMON") | Fraunces and Nunito only, sentence case |

## B. The bombs

| # | Flaw | Change |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | Every bomb skin is a blob: stair-stepped sides and random speckle (Ember's brown-and-orange noise, Peppermint's scattered green) | Clean round shells with bold patterns made by rules, every part at least 0.05 m: bands, cracks, belts (`T2`) |
| B2 | Skins differ almost only by colour. Only Plum's leaf and the Brass Bell have a shape of their own | Eight characters: a snow-capped Ice, a cracked Ember, a striped Peppermint, a Plum with stem and leaf, a belted Gold, a cut Diamond, a real Bell, a night-sky Aurora |
| B3 | The fuse is a fat pale stick with a white block on top | A twisted two-colour yarn fuse of 12 pieces, in each skin's own colours, with a lit tip and sparks |
| B4 | In a game the seconds are painted across the curve and break up | Every skin has a flat round face on its front; the seconds sit on it, whole. In the Trading Post the face shows 10, the bomb as a turn starts |
| B5 | A bomb you have not found is a washed-out beige picture with a lock | In a sack's line-up it is shown in full colour (you see what you could get). In the Barn Book it is a paper cut-out of its true shape |

## C. Sacks and the stall

| # | Flaw | Change |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | The sacks are dithered blobs with a pasted icon | Burlap sacks with woven rows, a yarn bow in the sack's colour and a stencilled patch (`T5`) |
| C2 | Scale, barrel and crates are mushy; the lantern hangs from nothing | A stall built of the game's own voxels: posts, a counter, a back wall of stock, a striped awning, lanterns on the front beam |
| C3 | "Feed Sacks, 250 each" sits on a black strip floating in the middle of the stall | "Feed Sacks" is the stall's sign; the price is on the button you press |
| C4 | The sack tags float on the stall front with a row of unexplained coloured squares | Each sack's paper tag hangs from the front beam: name and "Bomb skins: 2 of 5 found". The squares are gone; rarity is named on each item's own tag |
| C5 | "The odds" sits in a dashed dark box that runs off the panel | A chalk board standing beside the stall, all of it readable |

## D. Bursts and fuse sounds

| # | Flaw | Change |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | A burst is shown as a few floating specks (Starlight) or three lanterns hanging in the air (Lantern Glow) | Each burst is remodelled (`T3`) and shown at its fullest over a small stand; press it and it plays |
| D2 | Several bursts are clouds of loose cubes in many colours | At most 24 shaped pieces and three colours each |
| D3 | A fuse sound is a picture you cannot hear | Each is the thing that makes the sound (`T4`), with a Listen button |

## E. The Bell Shelf

| # | Flaw | Change |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Two items and four knobs marked "More soon": mostly empty wood | A stand with the two items and one small "More on the way" tag |
| E2 | The button says "30 more", which reads as a price | "15 more Bells" when you are short; "Buy" when you can |
| E3 | The rule about Bells is yellow handwriting on wood, and the line under it is cut off | One paper note under the stand, two lines, fully shown |

## F. Missions

| # | Flaw | Change |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | Mission cards mix a cream card, an orange picture frame and a gingham bar | Paper slips pinned to a cork board (`T6`) |
| F2 | The gingham bars look half full when they are empty | A knitted row that grows stitch by stitch; an empty bar is plainly empty |
| F3 | "0 / 7" for seven nights is a bar | Seven stamp boxes, one ticked per night |
| F4 | Coin and Bell rewards are yellow chips that look like buttons | An icon and "+25", not boxed |

## G. The Barn Book

| # | Flaw | Change |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | "3 / 26" beside two rows of dashed filter chips in capitals | A standing book with three bookmarks: Bomb skins, Bursts, Fuse sounds. One page spread shows a whole kind, so "found / not found" filters are not needed |
| G2 | A not-found card says "NOT FOUND YET" and "In the Oat Sack": the same thing twice | One line: where it comes from |
| G3 | Rarity is shown three ways: a green border, blue corner tabs, a gold glow | One way everywhere: a small coloured square and the word |
| G4 | "Wearing" is a green chip far below the picture | "Wearing" in green under the name; found items say "Found. Wear it" |

## H. Phones

| # | Flaw | Change |
|---|---|---|

Every rule and number (README, rule 1). The four sections and their names. The three sacks and what each holds. The level strip's facts. The footer line "Only for show…". The item preview's idea (a big view of one item with Wear), restyled in `T6`.
